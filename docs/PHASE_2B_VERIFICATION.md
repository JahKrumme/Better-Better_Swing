## Minimum standard and engine review

The existing evaluator already ignores numerical promotional amounts, estimated cash value and automation permission when deciding eligibility. The previous import incorrectly omitted `rewardTerms` evidence for rewards with unknown amounts, and RealPrize's playthrough passage was categorized as `playthroughRestrictions` rather than `playthroughRequirement`. Those were evidence-mapping errors, not proof that free rewards failed eligibility. RealPrize signup also lacked a primary one-time cadence record. We corrected those records using the actual clauses, retaining the old evidence.

One real over-coupling remained: a real Phase 2 offer always needed a known numerical redemption minimum and playthrough terms. A narrow correction now treats unknown redemption numbers as non-blocking **only when current primary evidence establishes both the prize mechanism and an affirmative purchase-free redemption path**. These uncertainties remain visible as value warnings. Without both proofs, the old checks still block. An explicitly described redemption mechanism also requires current purchase-free-path support, so Stake cannot qualify merely by later filling its minimum. All monetary-acquisition fields, fees, primary geography, age, expiry, freshness and conflict checks remain enforced. Legacy records and their tests retain their original behavior.

No redemption amount, monthly value, return rate or automation permission was invented. Known minimum/at-least playthrough terms remain subject to actual bonus-specific requirements and losses. Verification concerns the published free program; it is not a promise that a grant, a particular amount or cash proceeds will be available to every account.

## Opportunity and decision

- Verified known one-time promotional subtotal: **55 SC** from the distinct High 5 signup reward. This is neither $55 cash nor a guaranteed prize. RealPrize signup adds an unquantified potential grant.
- **Two verified recurring opportunities — value unquantified:** High 5 daily SC and RealPrize daily Sweep Coins. Cadence is one daily/24-hour opportunity respectively; nominal maxima are 7 claims/week and 30 claims/30-day month per program, assuming continuous availability and compliance. No SC/week or SC/month total is calculated without amounts; unknown is not zero.
- All estimated redeemable values remain **UNKNOWN**. Official conversion/threshold mechanics do not establish retained balances after promotional play, actual assigned multipliers or net proceeds after fees. Actual claims/redemptions recorded: 0.
- Manual requirements: register/maintain a free eligible account, perform the login/claim action, inspect any assigned requirements, play only the free promotional currency if seeking prizes, and complete the operator's identity/redemption process. No timing or actual claiming session was observed. The investigation performed none of these external actions.
- Existing automation findings are unchanged. High 5 prohibits automated play/redemption; Stake expressly prohibits automated acquisition; RealPrize prohibits game-play bots. None affirmatively authorizes automatic claiming. Narrow clauses are not rewritten as blanket daily-click bans. Across 20 candidates:0 PERMITTED,9 PROHIBITED,11 UNKNOWN.

**Was the old model too strict?** Partly: the data incorrectly coupled reward evidence to known amounts, and the evaluator blocked unknown numerical redemption conditions even when a complete purchase-free prize path could be proved. The correction preserves all core cost/location/evidence checks. None of the newly verified programs required removing an acquisition or geographic safeguard.

**Is Phase 3 justified now?** Not yet for automated monitoring or claiming. This is a stronger result than broad Phase 2: a 55 SC one-time lead plus two real daily programs can make the existing manual dashboard useful. Recurring amounts and achievable net returns still cannot establish the value of a monitoring build. No automatic-claim authorization exists. No Phase 3 work was started.

**Exact evidence that could change the decision:** actual current daily amounts and reset rules for High 5/RealPrize; their assigned bonus playthrough conditions; a reproducible eligible-user experience of grants and the no-purchase bank/gift-card prize path; measured manual effort; and sufficient supported recurring value to justify the implementation effort. Automated site collection or claiming would independently need official permission for the proposed actions. Resolving Stake could add a third lead but is not a prerequisite to evaluate the two verified daily programs.

**Can user-side manual inspection help?** Yes, if the user already has or independently chooses to use an eligible account: inspect the reward panel for credited amount, eligibility/claim cadence, the bonus-specific playthrough display, method-specific redemption minimum, fees, KYC requirements, and whether bank/gift-card redemption is available without prior purchase. For RealPrize signup, inspect the current grant and three-day condition. For Stake, an unlocked button alone does not resolve the terms ambiguity; seek an official no-purchase-account clarification. No password, cookie, session token, identity document or other authentication secret is requested or stored.
