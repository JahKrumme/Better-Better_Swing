## Automation policy scope (unchanged in Phase 2B)

**0 PERMITTED / 9 PROHIBITED / 11 UNKNOWN** across all20. The nine classifications retain minimal actual clauses:

- Stake.us: automated participation, **acquiring Stake Cash**, and play strictly prohibited; mail-entry automation also prohibited.
- PrizePicks: unauthorized scripts/automated accumulation of points, wins or prizes prohibited.
- High 5: automated/systematic play **and prize redemption** prohibited; game-play bots also prohibited. Daily-click scope is not separately resolved.

## Evidence and data handling

`research/access-results.json` retains the original failure receipts unchanged. `research/reviewed-findings.json` holds this manually reviewed static snapshot: 20 candidate summaries, minimal primary excerpts, timestamps, public-source access outcomes and 20 offer records after Phase 2B. Important known structured facts have corresponding source records; unknown facts stay null or explicitly described as unconfirmed. Failed/shell responses cannot verify an offer. Full HTML/PDF content was inspected temporarily outside the repository and is not included in the dataset.

The existing `pnpm research:import` now also imports the reviewed snapshot through `scripts/import-reviewed-research.ts`. This is **static data import, not a scraper or monitor**: no network calls, schedules, platform actions or claims. Validation enforces the candidate count, allowed fields, HTTPS primary origins and 1,000-character excerpt limit. Deterministic identities and insert-on-conflict behavior preserve evidence history, prevent duplicates and preserve manual offer edits. Later-dated platform reviews are not overwritten. Reimport does not refresh historical timestamps. Phase 2B adds a narrowly guarded distinction between existence and value quantification; stored metadata cannot force VERIFIED_FREE.

All changes remain uncommitted. Phase 3 has not started. The opportunity conclusion is cautious because actual reward conditions are incomplete, not because blocked brands were substituted or ignored.
